About QR Studio

QR Studio is a free, browser-based QR code generator built for individuals, creators, and small businesses. There's no account to create, nothing to install, and no server involved in generating your code — the QR is drawn directly on your device, using the same open-source engine each time you press download.

It supports the formats people actually need day to day: links, Wi-Fi credentials, contact cards, UPI payment requests, calendar invites, and more — each with sensible validation so you don't end up with a code that fails to scan.

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 5, 2026

QR Studio is built so that using it doesn't require trusting anyone with your data. This page explains exactly what happens — and just as importantly, what doesn't.

The short version

  • QR codes are generated entirely inside your browser. Whatever you type — a Wi-Fi password, a UPI ID, a phone number — is never sent to a server, because no server is involved in generating your code.
  • There's no account, no login, and nothing tied to your identity.
  • History and saved codes live only in your browser's local storage, on your own device. We never see them.
  • There's no analytics and no tracking script anywhere on this page.

What's stored, and where

When you save a QR code to History, or change a setting like your theme preference, that information is written to your browser's localStorage — a storage area built into the browser itself, scoped to this page, and never transmitted anywhere. It stays on your device until you delete it, either with the "Clear all" button in History or by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. It won't follow you to another browser or device, because it was never sent anywhere to begin with.

For QR types that can carry sensitive information — Wi-Fi passwords, contact details, UPI payment requests — QR Studio defaults "Don't save this QR to history" to on, so nothing is retained on-device unless you choose to keep it.

Third-party resources

This page loads a font library (Google Fonts) and two open-source code libraries used to draw and export QR codes (served from unpkg and cdnjs), so it doesn't have to ship everything itself. Loading any resource from the web means the provider serving it sees standard technical request information, like your IP address and browser type — that's true of any site using a CDN or web font, not something specific to QR Studio. None of these providers ever receive anything you type into the generator; that content never leaves your browser.

Clipboard access

The "Copy data" and "Copy image" buttons only touch your clipboard when you explicitly click them, to place the QR content or image there. QR Studio never reads your clipboard at any other time.

Cookies and tracking

QR Studio does not use cookies, does not run analytics, and does not include any advertising or tracking scripts.

Children's privacy

QR Studio doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone, of any age — it doesn't collect data at all. Everything described above stays on your own device, regardless of who's using it.

Changes to this policy

If how QR Studio works ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated along with the "Last updated" date above.

Frequently asked questions

Are QR codes free to generate?

Yes. Every feature in QR Studio — styling, logos, all export formats — is free, with no limits or watermarks.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the page and start generating. Nothing is tied to an identity or a login.

Does QR Studio store my data?

Only on your own device, and only if you choose to save a code to History. Nothing is transmitted to a server.

Can I use these QR codes commercially?

Yes. The QR codes you generate are standard, unencumbered QR codes — you're free to print and use them for business purposes.

Can I add my own logo?

Yes, from the Customize panel. QR Studio automatically increases error correction to help keep the code scannable once a logo is added.

Can I generate UPI QR codes?

Yes. QR Studio builds a standard UPI payment-request payload from your UPI ID, name, and optional amount. It only generates the code — it doesn't process or route any payment.

Can I print the QR codes I generate?

Yes. Export at a higher size (1024px or 2048px) for crisp printing, and keep a comfortable quiet zone around the code so scanners can find it easily.
